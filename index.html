Prototype notes for the Hush team
  • Prices, opening hours (08:00 to 21:00), pod availability and seat counts are sample data. Prices are in rupees as a placeholder only.
  • Call booths are booked in 30 or 60 minutes rather than 1, 2 or 4 hours, so one booking doesn’t hold a booth that other Callers need. Check this with real Callers.
  • Here, “Calls OK” is used for call booths only. Whether it’s a pod type, a zone or both is still undecided.
  • Crowd levels use sample thresholds. The real thresholds are still to decide.
  • Wordmark letter spacing is not final. Payment is not part of this prototype.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, with system fallbacks when offline.
